# Launch Marketing Campaign Workflow

1. **Define Goals & Audience**: Identify the core objectives of the campaign and the target audience.
2. **Select Channels**: Determine which marketing channels (e.g., email, social media, ads) will be used.
3. **Generate Assets**: Draft the necessary content (copy, images, landing pages).
4. **Review & Refine**: Request review from stakeholders to ensure brand alignment.
5. **Schedule & Deploy**: Execute the campaign on the selected channels.
